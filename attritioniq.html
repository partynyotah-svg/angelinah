    Help & guide

    Prepare workforce history for responsible group-level review.

    1 · Prepare a file

    Use one row per historical record, a header row, and a categorical outcome such as Attrition = Yes / No. CSV, TSV, and .xlsx files are supported.

    2 · Check and analyze

    Confirm the outcome and departure value. Choose a categorical grouping or explore a numeric field in bands. Blank outcomes are excluded.

    3 · Review patterns

    Groups and time periods under five records are withheld. Rates describe historical groups and do not establish why people left.

    4 · Plan at organization level

    Use comparisons and scenario estimates to review policies and programs. Do not use these outputs to make decisions about individual employees.

    ⓘ
    LimitationsThis local prototype has no HRIS integration, access management, or retention policy. Use synthetic data for demonstrations; follow your organization’s approved controls for real workforce data.
